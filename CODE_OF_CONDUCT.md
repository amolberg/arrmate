# Code of Conduct

Arrmate is a community project for people building and maintaining self-hosted media systems. Participation should be practical, welcoming, and respectful.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith.
- Give useful, specific feedback about code, UX, documentation, and security.
- Welcome newcomers and explain homelab-specific assumptions.
- Keep disagreements focused on the work, not the person.
- Respect privacy. Do not share credentials, private media metadata, private hostnames, or screenshots without permission.
- Follow the licenses and terms of the services Arrmate integrates with.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or targeted insults.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Credential theft, malicious code, destructive testing against systems you do not own, or instructions intended to bypass authorization.
- Deliberately filing deceptive reports or submitting code that hides dangerous side effects.
- Sexualized or otherwise inappropriate behavior in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when these standards are violated. Security issues should be reported privately according to `SECURITY.md`, not posted with working exploit details or secrets.

If enforcement is needed, provide maintainers with links, dates, and a concise description. Do not include private credentials or personal data.

This code of conduct is intentionally short. The standard is simple: build useful things, protect people and their data, and do not make the homelab community worse.

## Scope

This applies to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, project chat, and other spaces used for Arrmate collaboration.
